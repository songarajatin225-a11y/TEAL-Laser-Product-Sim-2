# Component models in the 3D view — credits and licence

The meshes in this folder were converted from STEP files in the FreeCAD parts library,
https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-library. That repository states: "All Parts in this
repository are licensed under CC-BY 3.0" (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Authors are taken from the library's commit history. The meshes were triangulated,
centred and quantized by `tools/cad3d/convert.mjs`; the shapes are otherwise unchanged.
